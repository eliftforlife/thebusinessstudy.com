How do car companies make money?

Carmakers sell vehicles to dealers (or, like Tesla, straight to buyers), and big trucks and SUVs carry most of the profit. Together, Ford, GM and Tesla took in $467 billion in their latest years and kept -$1.7 billion in profit, $-0.36 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.
