How do banks make money?

Banks pay you a little interest on deposits and lend that money out at a higher rate; the gap is their biggest source of income. Together, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and Goldman Sachs took in $438 billion in their latest years and kept $126 billion in profit, $28.81 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.
