Business statistics: what big companies earn and keep.

Numbers worked out from the latest annual reports of 102 big companies and the SEC's data on every U.S. public company. Free to cite.

102 big companiestook in $9.21 trillion in revenue in their latest years and kept $1.37 trillion in net profit.
$14.92is what they kept out of every $100 of sales, all together.
$12.42is what the typical (median) company kept of every $100.

How to cite: The Business Study, "Business statistics" (https://thebusinessstudy.com/business-statistics/), from companies' SEC filings, updated November 2026.

Sources: each company's Form 10-K (or 20-F) on SEC EDGAR, and the SEC's XBRL "frames" data for the largest companies. Profit = net income. See each company page for its filing.
