How do credit card companies make money?

There are two kinds. Together, Visa, American Express and Mastercard took in $120 billion in their latest years and kept $49.3 billion in profit, $41.09 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.

There are two kinds. Visa and Mastercard run the payment network and earn a small fee on every swipe, but they don't lend money. American Express runs a network and also issues cards, so it also earns card fees and interest from members.
