Dollar General vs Dollar Tree: who makes more money?

Dollar General is bigger: $42.7 billion in revenue against $19.4 billion for Dollar Tree, about 2.2 times as much. But Dollar Tree keeps more of each sale: $6.61 of every $100 versus $3.54. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Dollar General wins 4.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Dollar General or Dollar Tree?

Dollar General: $42.7 billion in revenue against $19.4 billion.

Which is more profitable, Dollar General or Dollar Tree?

Dollar Tree keeps more of each dollar ($6.61 of every $100). In total profit, Dollar General made more: $1.5 billion.

Which has more employees, Dollar General or Dollar Tree?

Dollar General, with about 194,000 against 153,032.
