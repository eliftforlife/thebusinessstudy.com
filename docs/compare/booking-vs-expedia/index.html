Booking.com vs Expedia: who makes more money?

Booking.com is bigger: $26.9 billion in revenue against $14.7 billion for Expedia, about 1.8 times as much. It also keeps more of each sale: $20.08 of every $100 versus $8.78. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Booking.com wins 5.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Booking.com or Expedia?

Booking.com: $26.9 billion in revenue against $14.7 billion.

Which is more profitable, Booking.com or Expedia?

Booking.com keeps more of each dollar ($20.08 of every $100). In total profit, Booking.com made more: $5.4 billion.

Which has more employees, Booking.com or Expedia?

Booking.com, with about 24,300 against 16,000.
