Visa vs Mastercard: who makes more money?

Visa is bigger: $45.9 billion in revenue against $32.8 billion for Mastercard, about 1.4 times as much. It also keeps more of each sale: $51.20 of every $100 versus $45.65. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Visa wins 4.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Visa or Mastercard?

Visa: $45.9 billion in revenue against $32.8 billion.

Which is more profitable, Visa or Mastercard?

Visa keeps more of each dollar ($51.20 of every $100). In total profit, Visa made more: $23.5 billion.

Which has more employees, Visa or Mastercard?

Mastercard, with about 39,800 against 34,800.
