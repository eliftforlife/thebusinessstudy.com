Apple is bigger: $479 billion in revenue against $332 billion for Microsoft, about 1.4 times as much. But Microsoft keeps more of each sale: $40.31 of every $100 versus $27.60. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Microsoft wins 3.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Apple or Microsoft?

Apple: $479 billion in revenue against $332 billion.

Which is more profitable, Apple or Microsoft?

Microsoft keeps more of each dollar ($40.31 of every $100). In total profit, Microsoft made more: $134 billion.

Which has more employees, Apple or Microsoft?

Microsoft, with about 223,000 against 167,000.
