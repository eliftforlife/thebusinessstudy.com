ExxonMobil vs Chevron: who makes more money?

ExxonMobil is bigger: $332 billion in revenue against $189 billion for Chevron, about 1.8 times as much. It also keeps more of each sale: $8.68 of every $100 versus $6.51. Across 4 rounds from their latest annual reports, ExxonMobil wins 3.
