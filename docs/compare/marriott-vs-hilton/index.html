Marriott vs Hilton: who makes more money?

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Marriott or Hilton?

Marriott: $26.2 billion in revenue against $12.0 billion.

Which is more profitable, Marriott or Hilton?

Hilton keeps more of each dollar ($12.10 of every $100). In total profit, Marriott made more: $2.6 billion.

Which has more employees, Marriott or Hilton?

Hilton, with about 182,000 against 148,000.
