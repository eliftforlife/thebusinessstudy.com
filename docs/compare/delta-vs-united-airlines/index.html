Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Delta or United Airlines?

Delta: $63.4 billion in revenue against $59.1 billion.

Which is more profitable, Delta or United Airlines?

Delta keeps more of each dollar ($7.90 of every $100). In total profit, Delta made more: $5.0 billion.

Which has more employees, Delta or United Airlines?

United Airlines, with about 113,200 against 103,000.
