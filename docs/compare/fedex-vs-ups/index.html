FedEx vs UPS: who makes more money?

FedEx is bigger: $94.7 billion in revenue against $88.7 billion for UPS, about 1.1 times as much. But UPS keeps more of each sale: $6.28 of every $100 versus $4.68. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, FedEx wins 3.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, FedEx or UPS?

FedEx: $94.7 billion in revenue against $88.7 billion.

Which is more profitable, FedEx or UPS?

UPS keeps more of each dollar ($6.28 of every $100). In total profit, UPS made more: $5.6 billion.

Which has more employees, FedEx or UPS?

UPS, with about 460,000 against 452,000.
