Amazon is bigger: $717 billion in revenue against $713 billion for Walmart, about 1.0 times as much. It also keeps more of each sale: $10.83 of every $100 versus $3.07. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Amazon wins 5.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Amazon or Walmart?

Amazon: $717 billion in revenue against $713 billion.

Which is more profitable, Amazon or Walmart?

Amazon keeps more of each dollar ($10.83 of every $100). In total profit, Amazon made more: $77.7 billion.

Which has more employees, Amazon or Walmart?

Walmart, with about 2,100,000 against 1,576,000.
