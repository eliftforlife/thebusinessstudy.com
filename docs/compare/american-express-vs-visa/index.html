American Express vs Visa: who makes more money?

Visa is bigger: $45.9 billion in revenue against $41.3 billion for American Express, about 1.1 times as much. It also keeps more of each sale: $51.20 of every $100 versus $26.23. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Visa wins 5.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, American Express or Visa?

Visa: $45.9 billion in revenue against $41.3 billion.

Which is more profitable, American Express or Visa?

Visa keeps more of each dollar ($51.20 of every $100). In total profit, Visa made more: $23.5 billion.

Which has more employees, American Express or Visa?

American Express, with about 76,800 against 34,800.
