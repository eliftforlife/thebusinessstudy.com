Verizon vs AT&T: who makes more money?

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Verizon or AT&T?

Verizon: $138 billion in revenue against $126 billion.

Which is more profitable, Verizon or AT&T?

AT&T keeps more of each dollar ($17.47 of every $100). In total profit, AT&T made more: $22.0 billion.

Which has more employees, Verizon or AT&T?

AT&T, with about 133,030 against 89,900.
