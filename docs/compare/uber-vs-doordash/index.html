Uber vs DoorDash: who makes more money?

Uber is bigger: $52.0 billion in revenue against $13.7 billion for DoorDash, about 3.8 times as much. It also keeps more of each sale: $19.33 of every $100 versus $6.82. Across 4 rounds from their latest annual reports, Uber wins 3.
