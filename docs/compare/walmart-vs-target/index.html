Walmart vs Target: who makes more money?

Walmart is bigger: $713 billion in revenue against $105 billion for Target, about 6.8 times as much. But Target keeps more of each sale: $3.54 of every $100 versus $3.07. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Walmart wins 4.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Walmart or Target?

Walmart: $713 billion in revenue against $105 billion.

Which is more profitable, Walmart or Target?

Target keeps more of each dollar ($3.54 of every $100). In total profit, Walmart made more: $21.9 billion.

Which has more employees, Walmart or Target?

Walmart, with about 2,100,000 against 415,000.
