Quick answers.

Who makes more money, AMD or Intel?

Intel: $52.9 billion in revenue against $34.6 billion.

Which is more profitable, AMD or Intel?

AMD keeps more of each dollar ($12.51 of every $100). In total profit, AMD made more: $4.3 billion.

Which has more employees, AMD or Intel?

Intel, with about 85,100 against 31,000.
