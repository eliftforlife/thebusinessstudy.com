Google is bigger: $403 billion in revenue against $201 billion for Meta, about 2.0 times as much. It also keeps more of each sale: $32.81 of every $100 versus $30.08. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Google wins 3.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, Google or Meta?

Google: $403 billion in revenue against $201 billion.

Which is more profitable, Google or Meta?

Google keeps more of each dollar ($32.81 of every $100). In total profit, Google made more: $132 billion.

Which has more employees, Google or Meta?

Google, with about 190,820 against 78,865.
