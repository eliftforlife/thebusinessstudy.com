T-Mobile vs Verizon: who makes more money?

Verizon is bigger: $138 billion in revenue against $88.3 billion for T-Mobile, about 1.6 times as much. But T-Mobile keeps more of each sale: $12.45 of every $100 versus $12.43. Across 5 rounds from their latest annual reports, Verizon wins 3.

Quick answers.

Who makes more money, T-Mobile or Verizon?

Verizon: $138 billion in revenue against $88.3 billion.

Which is more profitable, T-Mobile or Verizon?

T-Mobile keeps more of each dollar ($12.45 of every $100). In total profit, Verizon made more: $17.2 billion.

Which has more employees, T-Mobile or Verizon?

Verizon, with about 89,900 against 75,000.
