Home/Privacy

Privacy.

Short version: no accounts, no forms that collect personal data, and two kinds of visitor counting.

Website analytics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites, and with Google Analytics 4, which sets first-party cookies to tell visits apart and measure which pages people read. Google Analytics data is used only in aggregate (how many people visited which pages, from which country and which kind of device). You can block it with your browser's tracking protection or Google's opt-out add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Videos

YouTube videos load only when you press play (through youtube-nocookie.com). Until then no YouTube code runs on the page.

Games and votes

The higher-or-lower game keeps your best streak in your own browser (local storage); it never leaves your device. Votes on the vote page are counted without names or accounts.
