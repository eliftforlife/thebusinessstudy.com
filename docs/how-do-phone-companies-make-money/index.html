How do phone companies make money?

Phone carriers make most of their money from monthly plans, plus selling phones (often at little profit) and home internet. Together, Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile took in $352 billion in their latest years and kept $50.1 billion in profit, $14.23 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.
