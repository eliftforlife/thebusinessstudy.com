How do airlines make money?

Airlines sell seats, but tickets alone barely cover the cost of fuel, planes and crews. Together, Delta, United Airlines and Southwest took in $150 billion in their latest years and kept $8.8 billion in profit, $5.85 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.

Airlines sell seats, but tickets alone barely cover the cost of fuel, planes and crews. A big share of the profit comes from loyalty programs: banks pay airlines billions for miles they give out with credit cards. Bag and seat fees and cargo add the rest.
