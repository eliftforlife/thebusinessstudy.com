The Best Faceless YouTube Niches for Small Channels (572 Channels Ranked)

43×

more views per video for small true crime channels than small finance channels

By The Business Study · 2026-10-19 · 2 min read

The short answer

Among 572 small faceless channels (1,000 to 100,000 subscribers), true crime got the most views on a typical video: 14,000. Military history (13,500) and nostalgia (9,350) were next. Personal finance explainers got the fewest: 321. Stories beat advice.

01

Stories beat advice

The bottom of the ranking is mostly advice: money, books, motivation and psychology. The top is mostly real things that happened: crimes, wars and the past.

A story gives you a reason to keep watching. Advice is something you can skim, or get from a search result.

What to do

If you’re picking a faceless niche, start from stories about real events. If you love an advice niche, wrap the advice in a real story.

02

The “most profitable niche” earned the least

Finance pays the most per view (see what YouTube pays). But small finance channels barely got views. Say a finance video gets 321 views at $11.74: that’s about $3.77. A true crime video at 14,000 views and $4.75 is about $66.50.

That second RPM comes from a single true crime creator, so read it as an illustration, not an average.

What to do

Views times RPM is what pays. Pick the niche where small channels actually get watched, not the one with the highest RPM headline.

03

Some niches break out far more often

We counted how many channels had at least one video over 100,000 views in their last 30 uploads. In nostalgia it was 70% of channels. In royal family content it was 66%. In Reddit story channels it was 21%.

What to do

A niche where breakouts are common gives a small channel more lottery tickets. Nostalgia and royal content looked like the easiest places to get one.

04

Half of the “$30K a month” videos were selling something

We read the descriptions of faceless “I make $30,000 a month” videos. 25 of 48 were selling a course, coaching or a paid community.

What to do

Some of them really do make that money. But for a lot of them, the channel isn’t the business. You are.

How we did it

We searched 22 popular faceless niches and collected 2,320 channels, with each channel’s subscribers and its last 30 videos. We kept English, on-niche channels under 100,000 subscribers with at least 5 videos (742), and ranked the 572 between 1,000 and 100,000 subscribers so channel size is comparable. Niches with fewer than 8 channels were left out.

The limits

Channels were found through YouTube search, so they’re findable channels, not every channel. A median hides a wide spread, and some niches have only 9 to 14 channels.

Questions

What is the best faceless YouTube niche?

For small channels in this data: true crime (14,000 median views per video), then military history (13,500) and nostalgia (9,350).

Is faceless YouTube still worth it?

In story niches, small channels still get real views. In advice niches like finance explainers, book summaries and psychology facts, small channels struggled.

Is personal finance a good faceless niche?

It pays the most per view, but small finance channels got a median of 321 views per video, the fewest of any niche we ranked.
