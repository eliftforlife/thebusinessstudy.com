Viral Hooks: What 418 Breakout Shorts Said in the First 3 Seconds

2×

as many breakout Shorts said a real number in the first 3 seconds

By The Business Study · 2026-10-23

The short answer

Breakout Shorts said a real number in the first 3 seconds about twice as often as the same channel’s normal Shorts: 16.1% vs 8.3%. Opening on “you” was about half as common in breakouts. Seven popular hook formats, from questions to “the secret is”, made no difference.

01

A real number is the one hook that stood out

Breakouts opened with a concrete number, like a count, a time or a dollar amount, in 16.1% of cases. Normal Shorts from the same channels did it 8.3% of the time.

Real breakout openers: “For over 20 years, I have been taking photographs…” and “When the first settlers arrived over a thousand years ago…”. The effect leaned the same way in every topic group, and it was strongest in food, fitness and money.

What to do

Say a true, specific number before second 3. Don’t make one up: a number that isn’t real is the fastest way to lose a viewer.

02

Opening on “you” was less common in breakouts

Only 4.7% of breakouts opened with “you”, “your” or “if you”, against 8.5% of normal Shorts. Question titles followed the same pattern: 7.2% vs 12.3%.

What to do

Lead with the thing or your story, not with “You should…”. State it, don’t ask it.

03

Most famous hook tricks made no difference

Question hooks, “I” story openers, “here’s”, warnings, superlatives, “nobody” or “everyone”, and “the secret / the truth” all showed up about as often in breakouts as in normal Shorts, within about 1 point. Every call to action we checked (follow, subscribe, comment, part 2, save) was the same story.

What to do

Pick your call to action for what you want people to do, not for reach. Spend your effort on the first sentence instead.

04

Breakouts weren’t faster, just a little longer

In both groups the first word landed at 0.0 seconds and creators said about 10 words in the first 3 seconds. Breakouts ran 56 seconds against 50, at the same pace.

What to do

Start talking on frame one. You don’t need to talk faster, and a few extra seconds won’t hurt a Short that earns them.

How we did it

We searched 2,314 channels across 60 topics and kept 844 Shorts with captions: 418 breakouts (3× or more their channel’s median views) and 426 normal Shorts (0.7–1.3×) from the 113 channels that had both. The hook is the words spoken in the first 3 seconds. Shares are averaged per channel so big channels don’t dominate.

The limits

Auto-captions mis-hear some words and numbers, and 113 channels is a modest sample. This shows what breakouts have in common, not proof that a number causes a breakout. Test it on your own channel.

Questions

What is the best hook for YouTube Shorts?

In this data, a real, specific number in the first 3 seconds. Breakouts used one about twice as often as the same channel’s normal Shorts.

Do question hooks work on Shorts?

They made no difference here: 10.1% of breakouts and 9.9% of normal Shorts opened with a question.

Should Shorts start with “you”?

Breakouts did it about half as often (4.7% vs 8.5%). Lead with the thing itself.
