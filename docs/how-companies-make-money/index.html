How companies make money: 12 business models.

Almost every big company makes money one of a few ways. Here they are, with real companies and what each one keeps of every $100, from their annual reports.

01

Selling ads.

The product is free; advertisers pay to reach the people using it.

Together these 5 companies took in $616 billion and kept $193 billion: $31.34 of every $100.

Google$32.81
Meta$30.08
Reddit$24.05
Pinterest$9.87
Snapchat-$7.76

Kept per $100 of sales, latest annual report.
