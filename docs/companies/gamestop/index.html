Quick answers about GameStop.

How does GameStop make money?

GameStop sells game consoles, accessories, collectibles and new and used games. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Hardware and accessories ($1.8 billion).

How much money does GameStop make a year?

$3.6 billion in revenue in the year ended January 31, 2026, and $418 million in net profit. That's down 5% from the year before.

Is GameStop profitable?

Yes. GameStop made $418 million in net profit in the year ended January 31, 2026, which is $11.53 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020, 2021, 2022.

Is GameStop growing?

No. Revenue went from $5.1 billion in 2020 to $3.6 billion in 2025, down 29%.
