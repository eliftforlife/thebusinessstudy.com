eBay brought in $11.1 billion in revenue in 2025 and kept $2.0 billion as profit: $18.30 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Marketplace fees, at $9.1 billion (82% of the total).

Quick answers about eBay.

How does eBay make money?

eBay takes a cut of items sold on its marketplace and sells ads to sellers. It does not hold the inventory. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Marketplace fees ($9.1 billion).

How much money does eBay make a year?

$11.1 billion in revenue in 2025, and $2.0 billion in net profit. That's up 8% from the year before.

Is eBay profitable?

Yes. eBay made $2.0 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $18.30 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2022.

How many employees does eBay have?

About 12,300 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $902,439 in revenue per employee.

Is eBay growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $8.9 billion in 2020 to $11.1 billion in 2025, up 25%.
