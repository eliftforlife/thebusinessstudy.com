Quick answers about Cisco.

How does Cisco make money?

Cisco sells networking gear, security and collaboration software, and services.

How much money does Cisco make a year?

$63.3 billion in revenue in the year ended July 25, 2026, and $13.3 billion in net profit. That's up 12% from the year before.

Is Cisco profitable?

Yes. Cisco made $13.3 billion in net profit in the year ended July 25, 2026, which is $20.95 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Cisco have?

About 82,400 employees as of July 25, 2026. That works out to $768,507 in revenue per employee.

Is Cisco growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $49.8 billion in 2021 to $63.3 billion in 2026, up 27%.
