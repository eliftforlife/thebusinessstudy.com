Quick answers about AT&T.

How does AT&T make money?

AT&T sells phone plans, phones and fiber internet in the U.S., plus services for businesses.

How much money does AT&T make a year?

$126 billion in revenue in 2025, and $22.0 billion in net profit. That's up 3% from the year before.

Is AT&T profitable?

Yes. AT&T made $22.0 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $17.47 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020, 2022.

How many employees does AT&T have?

About 133,030 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $944,509 in revenue per employee.

Is AT&T growing?

No. Revenue went from $143 billion in 2020 to $126 billion in 2025, down 12%.
