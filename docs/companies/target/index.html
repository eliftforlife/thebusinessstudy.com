Quick answers about Target.

How does Target make money?

Target sells food, household goods, clothes, home and beauty products in its stores and online. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Food and drinks ($24.1 billion).

How much money does Target make a year?

$105 billion in revenue in the year ended January 31, 2026, and $3.7 billion in net profit. That's down 2% from the year before.

Is Target profitable?

Yes. Target made $3.7 billion in net profit in the year ended January 31, 2026, which is $3.54 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Target have?

About 415,000 full-time, part-time and seasonal team members as of January 31, 2026. That works out to $252,482 in revenue per employee.

Is Target growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $93.6 billion in 2020 to $105 billion in 2025, up 12%.
