Quick answers about Ford.

How does Ford make money?

Ford sells gas trucks and cars (Ford Blue), work vehicles and fleet services (Ford Pro), EVs (Model e), and makes car loans (Ford Credit). The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Ford Blue ($101 billion).

How much money does Ford make a year?

$187 billion in revenue in 2025, and $8.2 billion in net loss. That's up 1% from the year before.

Is Ford profitable?

No. Ford lost $8.2 billion in 2025.

Is Ford growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $127 billion in 2020 to $187 billion in 2025, up 47%.
