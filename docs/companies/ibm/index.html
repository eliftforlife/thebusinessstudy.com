Quick answers about IBM.

How does IBM make money?

IBM sells software (Red Hat, data and AI), consulting, and mainframes and other infrastructure. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Software ($30.0 billion).

How much money does IBM make a year?

$67.5 billion in revenue in 2025, and $10.6 billion in net profit. That's up 8% from the year before.

Is IBM profitable?

Yes. IBM made $10.6 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $15.69 of every $100 it took in.

Is IBM growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $55.2 billion in 2020 to $67.5 billion in 2025, up 22%.
