How Dollar General makes money

Dollar General brought in $42.7 billion in revenue in the year ended January 30, 2026 and kept $1.5 billion as profit: $3.54 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Consumables, at $35.1 billion (82% of the total).

Quick answers about Dollar General.

How does Dollar General make money?

Dollar General sells mostly everyday consumables in small stores close to rural and small-town shoppers. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Consumables ($35.1 billion).

How much money does Dollar General make a year?

$42.7 billion in revenue in the year ended January 30, 2026, and $1.5 billion in net profit. That's up 5% from the year before.

Is Dollar General profitable?

Yes. Dollar General made $1.5 billion in net profit in the year ended January 30, 2026, which is $3.54 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Dollar General have?

About 194,000 full-time and part-time employees as of February 27, 2026. That works out to $220,229 in revenue per employee.

Is Dollar General growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $33.7 billion in 2020 to $42.7 billion in 2025, up 27%.
