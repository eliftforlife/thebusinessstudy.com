Quick answers about Harley-Davidson.

How does Harley-Davidson make money?

Harley-Davidson sells motorcycles, parts and gear, and lends to buyers through its finance arm.

How much money does Harley-Davidson make a year?

$4.5 billion in revenue in 2025, and $339 million in net profit. That's down 14% from the year before.

Is Harley-Davidson profitable?

Yes. Harley-Davidson made $339 million in net profit in 2025, which is $7.57 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Harley-Davidson have?

About 5,500 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $813,305 in revenue per employee.

Is Harley-Davidson growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $4.1 billion in 2020 to $4.5 billion in 2025, up 10%.
