Quick answers about Zoom.

How does Zoom make money?

Zoom sells subscriptions to video meetings, phone and contact center software.

How much money does Zoom make a year?

$4.9 billion in revenue in the year ended January 31, 2026, and $1.9 billion in net profit. That's up 4% from the year before.

Is Zoom profitable?

Yes. Zoom made $1.9 billion in net profit in the year ended January 31, 2026, which is $39.03 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Zoom have?

About 7,438 full-time employees as of January 31, 2026. That works out to $654,580 in revenue per employee.

Is Zoom growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $2.7 billion in 2020 to $4.9 billion in 2025, up 84%.
