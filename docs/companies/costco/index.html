Quick answers about Costco.

How does Costco make money?

Costco sells goods at thin margins and makes much of its profit from membership fees. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Food and sundries ($117 billion).

How much money does Costco make a year?

$303 billion in revenue in the year ended August 30, 2026, and $9.2 billion in net profit. That's up 10% from the year before.

Is Costco profitable?

Yes. Costco made $9.2 billion in net profit in the year ended August 30, 2026, which is $3.04 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Costco have?

About 355,000 employees as of the end of fiscal 2026. That works out to $853,955 in revenue per employee.

Is Costco growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $196 billion in 2021 to $303 billion in 2026, up 55%.
