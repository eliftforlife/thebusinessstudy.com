Quick answers about Uber.

How does Uber make money?

Uber keeps a cut of every ride and delivery booked in its app. Drivers aren't employees, so the fares they keep aren't Uber's revenue. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Rides ($29.7 billion).

How much money does Uber make a year?

$52.0 billion in revenue in 2025, and $10.1 billion in net profit. That's up 18% from the year before.

Is Uber profitable?

Yes. Uber made $10.1 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $19.33 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020, 2021, 2022.

How many employees does Uber have?

About 34,000 employees (drivers are not employees) as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $1.53 million in revenue per employee.

Is Uber growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $11.1 billion in 2020 to $52.0 billion in 2025, up 367%.
