Quick answers about Broadcom.

How does Broadcom make money?

Broadcom sells chips for networking, AI and phones, and infrastructure software like VMware. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Chips ($36.9 billion).

How much money does Broadcom make a year?

$63.9 billion in revenue in the year ended November 2, 2025, and $23.1 billion in net profit. That's up 24% from the year before.

Is Broadcom profitable?

Yes. Broadcom made $23.1 billion in net profit in the year ended November 2, 2025, which is $36.20 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Broadcom have?

About 33,000 employees as of November 2, 2025. That works out to $1.94 million in revenue per employee.

Which part of Broadcom makes the most profit?

Chips, with $21.2 billion in operating profit in the year ended November 2, 2025.

Is Broadcom growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $23.9 billion in 2020 to $63.9 billion in 2025, up 167%.
