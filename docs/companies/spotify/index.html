Quick answers about Spotify.

How does Spotify make money?

Spotify makes most of its money from Premium subscriptions and the rest from ads on the free tier, and pays most of it to music rights holders. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Premium subscriptions ($15.3 billion).

How much money does Spotify make a year?

$17.2 billion in revenue in 2025, and $2.2 billion in net profit. That's up 10% from the year before.

Is Spotify profitable?

Yes. Spotify made $2.2 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $12.87 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023.

How many employees does Spotify have?

About 7,287 full-time employees on average as of 2025 (average). That works out to $2.36 million in revenue per employee.

Is Spotify growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $7.9 billion in 2020 to $17.2 billion in 2025, up 118%.
