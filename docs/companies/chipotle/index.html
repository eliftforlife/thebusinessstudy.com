Quick answers about Chipotle.

How does Chipotle make money?

Chipotle runs all of its own U.S. restaurants (no franchises), so its revenue is the food and drinks it sells.

How much money does Chipotle make a year?

$11.9 billion in revenue in 2025, and $1.5 billion in net profit. That's up 5% from the year before.

Is Chipotle profitable?

Yes. Chipotle made $1.5 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $12.88 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Chipotle have?

About 130,301 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $91,523 in revenue per employee.

Is Chipotle growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $6.0 billion in 2020 to $11.9 billion in 2025, up 99%.
