Quick answers about BlackRock.

How does BlackRock make money?

BlackRock charges small fees on the trillions of dollars it manages in funds and ETFs like iShares, plus its Aladdin software. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Fund management fees ($19.2 billion).

How much money does BlackRock make a year?

$24.2 billion in revenue in 2025, and $5.6 billion in net profit. That's up 19% from the year before.

Is BlackRock profitable?

Yes. BlackRock made $5.6 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $22.93 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does BlackRock have?

About 24,900 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $972,530 in revenue per employee.

Is BlackRock growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $17.9 billion in 2022 to $24.2 billion in 2025, up 35%.
