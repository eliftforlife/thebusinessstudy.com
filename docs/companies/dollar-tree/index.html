How Dollar Tree makes money

Dollar Tree brought in $19.4 billion in revenue in the year ended January 31, 2026 and kept $1.3 billion as profit: $6.61 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Consumables, at $9.4 billion (49% of the total).

Quick answers about Dollar Tree.

How does Dollar Tree make money?

Dollar Tree sells consumables and low-priced variety items. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Consumables ($9.4 billion).

How much money does Dollar Tree make a year?

$19.4 billion in revenue in the year ended January 31, 2026, and $1.3 billion in net profit. That's up 10% from the year before.

Is Dollar Tree profitable?

Yes. Dollar Tree made $1.3 billion in net profit in the year ended January 31, 2026, which is $6.61 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2023, 2024.

How many employees does Dollar Tree have?

About 153,032 full-time and part-time associates as of January 31, 2026. That works out to $126,848 in revenue per employee.

Is Dollar Tree growing?

No. Revenue went from $25.5 billion in 2020 to $19.4 billion in 2025, down 24%.
