Quick answers about Adobe.

How does Adobe make money?

Adobe sells subscriptions to Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Premiere), Acrobat and marketing software.

How much money does Adobe make a year?

$23.8 billion in revenue in the year ended November 28, 2025, and $7.1 billion in net profit. That's up 11% from the year before.

Is Adobe profitable?

Yes. Adobe made $7.1 billion in net profit in the year ended November 28, 2025, which is $30.00 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Adobe have?

About 31,360 employees as of November 28, 2025. That works out to $757,940 in revenue per employee.

Is Adobe growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $12.9 billion in 2020 to $23.8 billion in 2025, up 85%.
