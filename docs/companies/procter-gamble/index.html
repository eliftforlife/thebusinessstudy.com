How Procter & Gamble makes money

Procter & Gamble brought in $87.0 billion in revenue in the year ended June 30, 2026 and kept $16.0 billion as profit: $18.44 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Fabric and home care, at $30.3 billion (35% of the total).

Quick answers about Procter & Gamble.

How does Procter & Gamble make money?

Procter & Gamble sells household brands like Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Crest and Charmin. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Fabric and home care ($30.3 billion).

How much money does Procter & Gamble make a year?

$87.0 billion in revenue in the year ended June 30, 2026, and $16.0 billion in net profit. That's up 3% from the year before.

Is Procter & Gamble profitable?

Yes. Procter & Gamble made $16.0 billion in net profit in the year ended June 30, 2026, which is $18.44 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Procter & Gamble have?

About 104,000 employees as of June 30, 2026. That works out to $836,846 in revenue per employee.

Is Procter & Gamble growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $76.1 billion in 2021 to $87.0 billion in 2026, up 14%.
