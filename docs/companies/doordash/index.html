Quick answers about DoorDash.

How does DoorDash make money?

DoorDash earns fees from restaurants and stores on each order, delivery fees from customers, DashPass memberships and ads.

How much money does DoorDash make a year?

$13.7 billion in revenue in 2025, and $935 million in net profit. That's up 28% from the year before.

Is DoorDash profitable?

Yes. DoorDash made $935 million in net profit in 2025, which is $6.82 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023.

Is DoorDash growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $2.9 billion in 2020 to $13.7 billion in 2025, up 375%.
