How Lululemon makes money

Lululemon brought in $11.1 billion in revenue in the year ended February 1, 2026 and kept $1.6 billion as profit: $14.22 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Women's clothes, at $7.0 billion (63% of the total).

Quick answers about Lululemon.

How does Lululemon make money?

Lululemon sells athletic clothes, mostly through its own stores and website. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Women's clothes ($7.0 billion).

How much money does Lululemon make a year?

$11.1 billion in revenue in the year ended February 1, 2026, and $1.6 billion in net profit. That's up 5% from the year before.

Is Lululemon profitable?

Yes. Lululemon made $1.6 billion in net profit in the year ended February 1, 2026, which is $14.22 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Lululemon have?

About 39,000 employees as of February 1, 2026. That works out to $284,682 in revenue per employee.

Is Lululemon growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $4.4 billion in 2020 to $11.1 billion in 2025, up 152%.
