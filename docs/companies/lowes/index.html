How Lowe's makes money

Lowe's brought in $86.3 billion in revenue in the year ended January 30, 2026 and kept $6.7 billion as profit: $7.71 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Home decor, at $31.5 billion (37% of the total).

Quick answers about Lowe's.

How does Lowe's make money?

Lowe's sells home improvement products, from appliances and lumber to paint and garden supplies, mostly in the United States. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Home decor ($31.5 billion).

How much money does Lowe's make a year?

$86.3 billion in revenue in the year ended January 30, 2026, and $6.7 billion in net profit. That's up 3% from the year before.

Is Lowe's profitable?

Yes. Lowe's made $6.7 billion in net profit in the year ended January 30, 2026, which is $7.71 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Lowe's have?

About 276,000 associates (167,000 full-time and 109,000 part-time) as of January 30, 2026. That works out to $312,630 in revenue per employee.

Is Lowe's growing?

No. Revenue went from $89.6 billion in 2020 to $86.3 billion in 2025, down 4%.
