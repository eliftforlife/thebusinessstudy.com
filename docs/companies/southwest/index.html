Quick answers about Southwest.

How does Southwest make money?

Southwest sells airline tickets, plus loyalty program revenue, bag and seat fees, and cargo. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Tickets ($20.4 billion).

How much money does Southwest make a year?

$28.1 billion in revenue in 2025, and $441 million in net profit. That's up 2% from the year before.

Is Southwest profitable?

Yes. Southwest made $441 million in net profit in 2025, which is $1.57 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020.

Is Southwest growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $9.0 billion in 2020 to $28.1 billion in 2025, up 210%.
