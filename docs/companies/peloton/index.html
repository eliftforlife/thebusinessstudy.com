Quick answers about Peloton.

How does Peloton make money?

Peloton sells monthly class subscriptions and its bikes and treadmills. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Subscriptions ($1.7 billion).

How much money does Peloton make a year?

$2.4 billion in revenue in the year ended June 30, 2026, and $63 million in net profit. That's down 2% from the year before.

Is Peloton profitable?

Yes. Peloton made $63 million in net profit in the year ended June 30, 2026, which is $2.58 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025.

How many employees does Peloton have?

About 1,736 employees in the U.S. as of June 30, 2026. That works out to $1.41 million in revenue per employee.

Is Peloton growing?

No. Revenue went from $4.0 billion in 2021 to $2.4 billion in 2026, down 39%.
