How Qualcomm makes money

Quick answers about Qualcomm.

How does Qualcomm make money?

Qualcomm sells phone and car chips (QCT) and earns licensing fees on its wireless patents (QTL). The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Chips (QCT) ($37.2 billion).

How much money does Qualcomm make a year?

$43.1 billion in revenue in the year ended September 27, 2026, and $13.6 billion in net profit. That's down 3% from the year before.

Is Qualcomm profitable?

Yes. Qualcomm made $13.6 billion in net profit in the year ended September 27, 2026, which is $31.60 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Qualcomm have?

About 57,000 full-time, part-time and temporary workers as of September 27, 2026. That works out to $756,491 in revenue per employee.

Is Qualcomm growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $33.6 billion in 2021 to $43.1 billion in 2026, up 28%.
