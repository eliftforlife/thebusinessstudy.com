How Disney makes money

Disney brought in $94.4 billion in revenue in the year ended September 27, 2025 and kept $12.4 billion as profit: $13.14 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Entertainment, at $42.5 billion (44% of the total). But Parks and experiences made the most operating profit: $10.0 billion, 57% of Disney's $17.6 billion total.

Quick answers about Disney.

How does Disney make money?

Disney makes money from theme parks and cruises, streaming and TV (Disney+, Hulu, ESPN), movies, and toys and licensing. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Entertainment ($42.5 billion).

How much money does Disney make a year?

$94.4 billion in revenue in the year ended September 27, 2025, and $12.4 billion in net profit. That's up 3% from the year before.

Is Disney profitable?

Yes. Disney made $12.4 billion in net profit in the year ended September 27, 2025, which is $13.14 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020.

How many employees does Disney have?

About 231,000 employees as of fiscal year end 2025. That works out to $408,766 in revenue per employee.

Which part of Disney makes the most profit?

Parks and experiences, with $10.0 billion in operating profit in the year ended September 27, 2025.

Is Disney growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $65.4 billion in 2020 to $94.4 billion in 2025, up 44%.
