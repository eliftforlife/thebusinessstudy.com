Quick answers about Shopify.

How does Shopify make money?

Shopify charges stores a monthly subscription and earns most of its money from payments and other merchant services. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Merchant solutions ($8.8 billion).

How much money does Shopify make a year?

$11.6 billion in revenue in 2025, and $1.2 billion in net profit. That's up 30% from the year before.

Is Shopify profitable?

Yes. Shopify made $1.2 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $10.65 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2022.

How many employees does Shopify have?

About 7,600 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $1.52 million in revenue per employee.

Is Shopify growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $2.9 billion in 2020 to $11.6 billion in 2025, up 294%.
