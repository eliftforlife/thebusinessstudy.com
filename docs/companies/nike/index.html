How Nike makes money

Quick answers about Nike.

How does Nike make money?

Nike sells shoes, clothes and gear through stores like Foot Locker and through its own stores and app. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Shoes ($30.5 billion).

How much money does Nike make a year?

$46.4 billion in revenue in the year ended May 31, 2026, and $3.1 billion in net profit. That's up 0% from the year before.

Is Nike profitable?

Yes. Nike made $3.1 billion in net profit in the year ended May 31, 2026, which is $6.70 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Nike have?

About 73,000 employees, including retail and part-time as of May 31, 2026. That works out to $635,589 in revenue per employee.

Is Nike growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $44.5 billion in 2020 to $46.4 billion in 2025, up 4%.
