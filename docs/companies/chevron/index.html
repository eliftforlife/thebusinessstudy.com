Quick answers about Chevron.

How does Chevron make money?

Chevron finds and pumps oil and gas, and refines and sells fuel and lubricants.

How much money does Chevron make a year?

$189 billion in revenue in 2025, and $12.3 billion in net profit. That's down 7% from the year before.

Is Chevron profitable?

Yes. Chevron made $12.3 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $6.51 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020.

Is Chevron growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $94.7 billion in 2020 to $189 billion in 2025, up 100%.
