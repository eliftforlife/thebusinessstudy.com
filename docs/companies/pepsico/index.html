PepsiCo, Inc. · PEP

How PepsiCo makes money

PepsiCo brought in $93.9 billion in revenue in the year ended December 27, 2025 and kept $8.2 billion as profit: $8.77 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Drinks, North America, at $28.2 billion (30% of the total). But Snacks, North America made the most operating profit: $6.2 billion, 54% of PepsiCo's $11.5 billion total.

Quick answers about PepsiCo.

How does PepsiCo make money?

PepsiCo sells snacks (Lay's, Doritos, Quaker) and drinks (Pepsi, Gatorade), and makes more profit from snacks than from soda. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Drinks, North America ($28.2 billion).

How much money does PepsiCo make a year?

$93.9 billion in revenue in the year ended December 27, 2025, and $8.2 billion in net profit. That's up 2% from the year before.

Is PepsiCo profitable?

Yes. PepsiCo made $8.2 billion in net profit in the year ended December 27, 2025, which is $8.77 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does PepsiCo have?

About 306,000 employees as of December 27, 2025. That works out to $306,944 in revenue per employee.

Which part of PepsiCo makes the most profit?

Snacks, North America, with $6.2 billion in operating profit in the year ended December 27, 2025.

Is PepsiCo growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $70.4 billion in 2020 to $93.9 billion in 2025, up 33%.
