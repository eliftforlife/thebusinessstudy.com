Quick answers about GM.

How does GM make money?

GM makes almost all its money selling cars and trucks, plus parts, services and car loans through GM Financial. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is GM North America ($154 billion).

How much money does GM make a year?

$185 billion in revenue in 2025, and $2.7 billion in net profit. That's down 1% from the year before.

Is GM profitable?

Yes. GM made $2.7 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $1.46 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does GM have?

About 156,000 employees (88,000 hourly and 68,000 salaried) as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $1.19 million in revenue per employee.

Is GM growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $122 billion in 2020 to $185 billion in 2025, up 51%.
