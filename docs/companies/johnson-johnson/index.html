Quick answers about Johnson & Johnson.

How does Johnson & Johnson make money?

Johnson & Johnson sells prescription medicines and medical devices. Its consumer brands split off as Kenvue in 2023. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Medicines ($60.4 billion).

How much money does Johnson & Johnson make a year?

$94.2 billion in revenue in the year ended December 28, 2025, and $26.8 billion in net profit. That's up 6% from the year before.

Is Johnson & Johnson profitable?

Yes. Johnson & Johnson made $26.8 billion in net profit in the year ended December 28, 2025, which is $28.46 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Johnson & Johnson have?

About 140,800 employees as of December 28, 2025. That works out to $668,984 in revenue per employee.

Is Johnson & Johnson growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $82.6 billion in 2020 to $94.2 billion in 2025, up 14%.
