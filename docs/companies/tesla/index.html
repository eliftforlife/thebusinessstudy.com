Quick answers about Tesla.

How does Tesla make money?

Tesla makes most of its money selling electric cars, plus batteries for homes and power grids, service, and credits it sells to other carmakers. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Car sales ($65.8 billion).

How much money does Tesla make a year?

$94.8 billion in revenue in 2025, and $3.8 billion in net profit. That's down 3% from the year before.

Is Tesla profitable?

Yes. Tesla made $3.8 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $4.00 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Tesla have?

About 134,785 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $703,543 in revenue per employee.

Is Tesla growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $31.5 billion in 2020 to $94.8 billion in 2025, up 201%.
