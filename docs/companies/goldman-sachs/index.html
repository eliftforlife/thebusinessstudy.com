Quick answers about Goldman Sachs.

How does Goldman Sachs make money?

Goldman Sachs earns fees for advising on deals and raising money, trading for clients, and managing money. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Banking and markets ($41.5 billion).

How much money does Goldman Sachs make a year?

$58.3 billion in net revenue in 2025, and $17.2 billion in net profit. That's up 9% from the year before.

Is Goldman Sachs profitable?

Yes. Goldman Sachs made $17.2 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $29.47 of every $100 it took in.

Is Goldman Sachs growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $44.6 billion in 2020 to $58.3 billion in 2025, up 31%.
