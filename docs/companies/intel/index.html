How Intel makes money

Intel brought in $52.9 billion in revenue in the year ended December 27, 2025 and lost $267 million as a net loss: $0.51 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was PC chips, at $32.2 billion (61% of the total). Intel Foundry lost $10.3 billion.

Quick answers about Intel.

How does Intel make money?

Intel sells processors for PCs and data centers and runs a chip factory business, Intel Foundry, that is losing money. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is PC chips ($32.2 billion).

How much money does Intel make a year?

$52.9 billion in revenue in the year ended December 27, 2025, and $267 million in net loss. That's down 0% from the year before.

Is Intel profitable?

No. Intel lost $267 million in the year ended December 27, 2025.

How many employees does Intel have?

About 85,100 employees as of December 27, 2025. That works out to $621,069 in revenue per employee.

Which part of Intel makes the most profit?

PC chips, with $9.3 billion in operating profit in the year ended December 27, 2025.

Is Intel growing?

No. Revenue went from $77.9 billion in 2020 to $52.9 billion in 2025, down 32%.
