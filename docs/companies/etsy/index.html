Quick answers about Etsy.

How does Etsy make money?

Etsy takes a cut of every sale on its marketplace and sells ads and services to sellers. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Marketplace fees ($2.0 billion).

How much money does Etsy make a year?

$2.9 billion in revenue in 2025, and $163 million in net profit. That's up 3% from the year before.

Is Etsy profitable?

Yes. Etsy made $163 million in net profit in 2025, which is $5.65 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2022.

How many employees does Etsy have?

About 2,375 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $1.21 million in revenue per employee.

Is Etsy growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $1.7 billion in 2020 to $2.9 billion in 2025, up 67%.
