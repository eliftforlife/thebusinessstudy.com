How Domino's makes money

Domino's brought in $4.9 billion in revenue in the year ended December 28, 2025 and kept $602 million as profit: $12.18 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Selling supplies to franchisees, at $3.0 billion (61% of the total).

Quick answers about Domino's.

How does Domino's make money?

Domino's earns most of its revenue selling dough, cheese and supplies to its own franchisees, plus royalties and its stores' sales. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Selling supplies to franchisees ($3.0 billion).

How much money does Domino's make a year?

$4.9 billion in revenue in the year ended December 28, 2025, and $602 million in net profit. That's up 5% from the year before.

Is Domino's profitable?

Yes. Domino's made $602 million in net profit in the year ended December 28, 2025, which is $12.18 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Domino's have?

About 10,200 employees (franchised stores hire their own staff) as of December 28, 2025. That works out to $484,313 in revenue per employee.

Is Domino's growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $4.1 billion in 2020 to $4.9 billion in 2025, up 20%.
