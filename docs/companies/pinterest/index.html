Quick answers about Pinterest.

How does Pinterest make money?

Pinterest makes its money from ads.

How much money does Pinterest make a year?

$4.2 billion in revenue in 2025, and $417 million in net profit. That's up 16% from the year before.

Is Pinterest profitable?

Yes. Pinterest made $417 million in net profit in 2025, which is $9.87 of every $100 it took in. It lost money in 2020, 2022, 2023.

How many employees does Pinterest have?

About 5,265 full-time employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $801,855 in revenue per employee.

Is Pinterest growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $1.7 billion in 2020 to $4.2 billion in 2025, up 149%.
