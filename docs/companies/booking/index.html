Quick answers about Booking.com.

How does Booking.com make money?

Booking Holdings (Booking.com, Priceline, Kayak) earns commissions on hotel and travel bookings. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Merchant bookings ($17.8 billion).

How much money does Booking.com make a year?

$26.9 billion in revenue in 2025, and $5.4 billion in net profit. That's up 13% from the year before.

Is Booking.com profitable?

Yes. Booking.com made $5.4 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $20.08 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Booking.com have?

About 24,300 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $1.11 million in revenue per employee.

Is Booking.com growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $6.8 billion in 2020 to $26.9 billion in 2025, up 296%.
