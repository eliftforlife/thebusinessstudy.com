Quick answers about McDonald's.

How does McDonald's make money?

McDonald's mostly earns rent and royalties from franchisees who run the restaurants, plus sales from the restaurants it runs itself. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Big international markets ($13.6 billion).

How much money does McDonald's make a year?

$26.9 billion in revenue in 2025, and $8.6 billion in net profit. That's up 4% from the year before.

Is McDonald's profitable?

Yes. McDonald's made $8.6 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $31.85 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does McDonald's have?

About 150,000 company employees (franchised restaurants hire their own staff) as of year-end 2025. That works out to $179,233 in revenue per employee.

Which part of McDonald's makes the most profit?

Big international markets, with $6.4 billion in operating profit in 2025.

Is McDonald's growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $19.2 billion in 2020 to $26.9 billion in 2025, up 40%.
