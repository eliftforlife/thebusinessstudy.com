How Darden Restaurants makes money

Darden Restaurants brought in $13.2 billion in revenue in the year ended May 31, 2026 and kept $1.2 billion as profit: $9.13 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Olive Garden, at $5.6 billion (42% of the total).

Quick answers about Darden Restaurants.

How does Darden Restaurants make money?

Darden runs Olive Garden, LongHorn Steakhouse and other chains, mostly as company-owned restaurants. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Olive Garden ($5.6 billion).

How much money does Darden Restaurants make a year?

$13.2 billion in revenue in the year ended May 31, 2026, and $1.2 billion in net profit. That's up 9% from the year before.

Is Darden Restaurants profitable?

Yes. Darden Restaurants made $1.2 billion in net profit in the year ended May 31, 2026, which is $9.13 of every $100 it took in.

Is Darden Restaurants growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $7.2 billion in 2020 to $13.2 billion in 2025, up 84%.
