How Microsoft makes money

Microsoft brought in $332 billion in revenue in the year ended June 30, 2026 and kept $134 billion as profit: $40.31 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was Office, LinkedIn, Dynamics, at $140 billion (42% of the total).

Quick answers about Microsoft.

How does Microsoft make money?

Microsoft rents cloud computing (Azure), sells Office and LinkedIn subscriptions, and sells Windows, Xbox and Surface. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Office, LinkedIn, Dynamics ($140 billion).

How much money does Microsoft make a year?

$332 billion in revenue in the year ended June 30, 2026, and $134 billion in net profit. That's up 18% from the year before.

Is Microsoft profitable?

Yes. Microsoft made $134 billion in net profit in the year ended June 30, 2026, which is $40.31 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Microsoft have?

About 223,000 full-time employees as of June 30, 2026. That works out to $1.49 million in revenue per employee.

Which part of Microsoft makes the most profit?

Office, LinkedIn, Dynamics, with $83.9 billion in operating profit in the year ended June 30, 2026.

Is Microsoft growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $168 billion in 2021 to $332 billion in 2026, up 97%.
