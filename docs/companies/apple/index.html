Apple Inc. · AAPL

How Apple makes money

Apple brought in $479 billion in revenue in the year ended September 26, 2026 and kept $132 billion as profit: $27.60 of every $100. Its biggest source of money was iPhone, at $254 billion (53% of the total). But Americas made the most operating profit: $86.0 billion, 54% of Apple's $158 billion total.

Quick answers about Apple.

How does Apple make money?

Apple sells iPhones, Macs, iPads and accessories, and keeps a big share of every dollar from Services like the App Store and iCloud. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is iPhone ($254 billion).

How much money does Apple make a year?

$479 billion in revenue in the year ended September 26, 2026, and $132 billion in net profit. That's up 15% from the year before.

Is Apple profitable?

Yes. Apple made $132 billion in net profit in the year ended September 26, 2026, which is $27.60 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Apple have?

About 167,000 full-time equivalent employees as of September 26, 2026. That works out to $2.87 million in revenue per employee.

Which part of Apple makes the most profit?

Americas, with $86.0 billion in operating profit in the year ended September 26, 2026.

Is Apple growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $366 billion in 2021 to $479 billion in 2026, up 31%.
