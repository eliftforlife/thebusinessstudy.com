How Monster Energy makes money

Quick answers about Monster Energy.

How does Monster Energy make money?

Monster sells energy drinks to bottlers and distributors, mostly Coca-Cola bottlers. The biggest line in its latest 10-K is Monster energy drinks ($7.7 billion).

How much money does Monster Energy make a year?

$8.3 billion in revenue in 2025, and $1.9 billion in net profit. That's up 11% from the year before.

Is Monster Energy profitable?

Yes. Monster Energy made $1.9 billion in net profit in 2025, which is $22.97 of every $100 it took in.

How many employees does Monster Energy have?

About 6,891 employees as of December 31, 2025. That works out to $1.20 million in revenue per employee.

Is Monster Energy growing?

Yes. Revenue went from $4.6 billion in 2020 to $8.3 billion in 2025, up 80%.
