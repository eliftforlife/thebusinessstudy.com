How do social media companies make money?

Social apps are free because advertisers pay to show ads to the people scrolling. Together, Meta, Snapchat, Pinterest and Reddit took in $213 billion in their latest years and kept $60.9 billion in profit, $28.57 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.
