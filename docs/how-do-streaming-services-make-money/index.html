How do streaming services make money?

Streaming services charge a monthly subscription, and more of them now sell cheaper plans with ads. Together, Disney, Netflix and Spotify took in $157 billion in their latest years and kept $25.6 billion in profit, $16.33 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.
