How do hotel companies make money?

The biggest hotel companies own very few hotels. Together, Marriott and Hilton took in $38.2 billion in their latest years and kept $4.1 billion in profit, $10.62 of every $100.

From each company's latest Form 10-K. Page updated November 8, 2026.

The biggest hotel companies own very few hotels. They manage or franchise hotels owned by investors and charge fees on room revenue, plus loyalty and credit card money.
