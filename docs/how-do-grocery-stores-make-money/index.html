How do grocery stores make money?

Grocers keep only pennies per dollar of sales, so they win by volume, store brands, pharmacy, fuel and, at Costco and Sam's Club, membership fees.. Together, Walmart, Costco, Kroger, Target and Dollar General took in $1.31 trillion in their latest years and kept $37.4 billion in profit, $2.85 of every $100.
